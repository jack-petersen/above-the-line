Privacy Policy

Above the Line — Rubber Bridge Scorer

Last updated August 17, 2026

Above the Line does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There's no account system, no server, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

The app stores what you enter directly into it — player names, rubber and hand records, contracts, and scores — in a local database on your device. This data is used only to run the app's scoring and analytics features for you.

What the app doesn't do

  • It doesn't send your data anywhere — there's no server, and the app doesn't require an internet connection to work.
  • It doesn't use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising services.
  • It doesn't access your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.
  • It doesn't create accounts or ask for your name, email, or any other identifying information.

Export and deletion

You can export your data (CSV/JSON) from within the app for your own backup. Deleting the app from your device permanently deletes all of its data, since nothing is retained anywhere else.

Children's privacy

Above the Line isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it doesn't collect data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version adds optional cloud backup — this page will be updated and the date above will change accordingly.
