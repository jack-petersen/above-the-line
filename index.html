Above the Line

A scorepad for in-person rubber bridge, built for people who already know how to play and just want the arithmetic (and the memory) handled for you.

At the table

Enter each hand fast: contract, declarer, doubled or redoubled, made or down. Vulnerability, game legs, and the rubber bonus are all tracked automatically, correctly, hand to hand, including the parts other scoring apps get wrong, like carrying vulnerability across games within a rubber.

After the table

This is where Above the Line is different. Every rubber you play quietly builds a picture of how you actually play.

  • Win rate and margin by partner, and by opponent
  • Trends over time, not just a single session's tally
  • Doubled-contract record, slam attempts, declarer vs. defender performance
  • Contract frequency: which bids you make most, and how often they hold up

No competitor tracks any of this. Most stop at a single game's scoresheet.

No account, no sign-in, no server. Your games live on your device.
