Frequently asked questions

Is my data private?

Yes. Above the Line has no account system and no server — every rubber, hand, and stat is stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded or shared.

Does it sync across devices?

Not currently. Your games live on the device you enter them on. Cross-device sync isn't part of the app today.

Can it help me bid or play a hand?

No. Above the Line is a scorekeeping companion for a real, in-person game played with real cards at a real table — it doesn't simulate bidding or play.

What bridge variants are supported?

Rubber bridge only. Duplicate and tournament formats aren't part of the app.

Which platforms is it available on?

iPhone, via the App Store.
